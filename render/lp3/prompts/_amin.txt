THE CHARACTER — the small green car in the reference image, exactly as it appears there. An ordinary everyday Egyptian FOUR-DOOR SALOON: three-box, a clear separate boot at the back, a long flat bonnet, four side windows, a level roofline, soft rounded friendly forms. Never a short, tall, round two-door bubble hatchback; it always has a boot. It is a FULL-SIZE ROAD CAR, the same size as the cars around it — never a toy, never a miniature. Its paint is flat matte vivid spring-green, one uniform saturated colour, chalky and non-glossy — never pale pastel mint. Plain grey five-spoke steel wheels.

ITS FACE — large round expressive eyes set INSIDE THE WINDSHIELD GLASS, warm dark brown irises, bright and cheerful. Its mouth is its GRILLE: a large recessed opening low in the front, a wide crescent whose ends sweep upward so the opening itself reads as a warm closed-mouth smile. Directly below the grille sits a blank number-plate recess, so the nose is fully occupied: there is NO smooth bumper below the grille and NO second smiling crease pressed into the paint. The headlamps are plain lamps and are never eyes.

ITS STRIPES — two narrow flat white bands run dead centre along the top of the car, straddling its exact centreline, straight from bonnet to roof to boot lid, parallel and of equal constant width. Never angled, never swept, never offset.

ITS MARKINGS — the wordmark "140 Auto" in warm off-white, both words together on ONE line, in plain flat lettering, and TINY: no taller than a door handle, a small fraction of the panel it sits on. On the bonnet and low on each front door. Never large, never bold, never stacked, never the number alone, never in a circle or roundel, never a race number or emblem. Number-plate recesses are empty. No other text, numerals, decals or stickers on the car.

WHERE ITS FACE IS — the face exists ONLY on the front. The eyes are in the FRONT windshield and nowhere else. The rear windscreen, the boot lid, the rear bumper and every side window are plain ordinary glass and bodywork with NO eyes, NO mouth and NO face. With the camera behind the car we see only its plain back, with no face visible at all.

ONLY THIS CAR IS ALIVE. No other vehicle has eyes, a face, a mouth or any expression. Every other car's grille is a PLAIN STRAIGHT FLAT RECTANGLE of slats with square corners — it never curves, arches or reads as a smile, and no other car has any grin or smiling crease anywhere on its bumper or bodywork. The crescent smiling grille belongs to the little green car alone. Every other car is an ordinary lifeless saloon of the SAME SIZE as it, never larger, never towering over it.
