PORTRAIT COMPOSITION — this is a TALL 9:16 vertical frame, composed for a phone from scratch. It is NOT a crop of a wide shot and must not look like one. Recompose the whole scene vertically while keeping the camera rule exactly: still bolted down, still square-on and frontal, still level, still at the same standing-eye height, with the horizon still dead flat and horizontal. Do not tilt up, do not tilt down, do not switch to a low or high angle to fit the tall frame.

What changes is only the framing: bring the camera closer to the subject so it fills the narrow width and reads clearly at phone size, place the horizon line a little below the vertical middle, and let the pale open sky occupy the upper part of the frame. The main subject sits centred on the vertical axis. Anything that ran off to the left and right in the wide version — a long row of units, a line of plinths — is simply shown closer and shorter, with fewer elements, rather than squeezed. Nothing important sits near the left or right edges, because there is very little width to work with.
