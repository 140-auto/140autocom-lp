SCENE — the opening frame. A wide, clean, empty stretch of new tarmac on the edge of a Cairo suburb in the early morning. Behind it, far back and small, a low line of pale concrete apartment blocks with balconies and a few palm trees, softened by distance. The road surface is smooth and freshly swept. There is nothing else in the frame: no other vehicles, no people, no signage, no clutter. The sky is a pale neutral off-white haze.

The little green car is parked exactly in the centre of the frame, square-on to the camera, facing straight down the lens, both eyes visible and looking directly forward. It is small in the frame with plenty of clean empty tarmac around it and plenty of open sky above it — a calm, confident, poster-like opening composition with generous negative space.
