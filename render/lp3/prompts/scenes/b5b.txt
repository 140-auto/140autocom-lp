SCENE — a quiet, clean residential street in a Cairo suburb, seen dead-on. A short paved driveway in front of a low pale apartment block with balconies; a palm tree to one side; clean kerbs. One ordinary grey family saloon is parked on the driveway, square-on to the camera, in good clean condition. It is an ordinary lifeless vehicle with plain blank headlights and no face. Bright, clean midday daylight. No people, no dust, no clutter, no dust sheets or covers of any kind.

The camera is level and square-on to the driveway; the grey car stays in exactly the same place in both frames.

The blank sign board is GONE from the windscreen — the windscreen is clear and empty. The little green car has arrived and is parked at the kerb in front of the driveway, in three-quarter view, facing the camera. It stays well inside the frame, clear of every edge, and no larger in frame than the grey car.
