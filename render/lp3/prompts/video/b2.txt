The camera is completely locked off on a tripod and does not move at all: no pan, no tilt, no push in, no pull back, no zoom, no handheld drift, no shake. The framing is identical from the first frame to the last.

The grey car in the centre of the bay stays perfectly still and does not move at all. The engineer standing at its front left corner stays exactly where he is throughout — he does not walk, does not change position and does not leave the frame; he only shifts his weight slightly and keeps looking calmly down at the car. The bonnet of the grey car rises smoothly open part way through the shot.

The only travelling motion is the little green car, which drives smoothly and steadily around the FAR side of the grey car in one continuous unbroken arc, from the left of the bay round behind it to the right of the bay, at a slow deliberate pace. It turns gradually and continuously through the whole arc, always following its own nose — it never spins on the spot, never jumps, and never abruptly faces a new direction. Its headlamps sweep a soft pool of light across the grey car's bodywork as it goes.
