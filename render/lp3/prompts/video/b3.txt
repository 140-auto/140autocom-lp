The camera is completely locked off on a tripod and does not move at all: no pan, no tilt, no push in, no pull back, no zoom, no handheld drift, no shake. The framing is identical from the first frame to the last.

The row of plinths, gantries and grey cars is completely static and nothing about it changes. No people appear.

The only motion is the little green car, which is seen from behind throughout, driving smoothly and steadily away from the camera along the front of the row, from left of centre to right of centre, at a calm even pace. It stays pointing away from the camera for the entire shot. It never turns round, never reverses, never spins and never comes to face the camera. We see only its plain back the whole time and no face is ever visible on it.
