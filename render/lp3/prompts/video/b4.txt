The camera is completely locked off on a tripod and does not move at all: no pan, no tilt, no push in, no pull back, no zoom, no handheld drift, no shake. The framing is identical from the first frame to the last. Nothing in the background moves and no people appear.

The little green car stays exactly where it is for the whole shot, parked square-on and facing the camera. It does not drive, does not roll, does not turn and does not leave its spot. It is simply standing there, calm and pleased.

The only thing that changes is the STACK OF PAPER on the ground to the right of it, which GROWS: sheet after sheet settles neatly onto the top of the pile, one after another in a steady rhythm, so the tidy tower rises smoothly from a thin pile of a few sheets at the start to a tall stack about as high as the car's roof at the end. The stack stays in exactly the same spot on the ground, stays neat, and never topples, fans out or scatters. Every sheet stays covered in its handwritten cursive scribble.
