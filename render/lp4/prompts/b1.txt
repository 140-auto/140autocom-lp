STYLE — identical in every scene of this film. Soft matte 3D animation in the style of a modern animated feature. Every surface is flat and chalky with no gloss, no specular highlights, no reflections and no metallic sheen. Gentle even ambient light, soft contact shadows, very slight tilt-shift depth. Simplified friendly geometry — clean shapes, no clutter of tiny detail, no photorealism.

COLOUR LAW — obeyed absolutely. The ONLY saturated colour anywhere in the frame is the green of the little green car. Everything else in the world is strictly desaturated: deep navy, dark slate grey, warm off-white paper, and medium neutral grey. No blue sky, no orange sunset, no red, no yellow, no colourful signage, no colourful clothing, no coloured lights. Other vehicles are grey. Buildings are grey and off-white. The sky is a pale neutral off-white haze. Nothing competes with the green car.

PLACE — recognisably Egyptian. Cairo road architecture, concrete flyovers on round columns, dusty kerbs, palm trees, low concrete apartment blocks with balconies, desert-edge ring roads.

STRICTLY FORBIDDEN — no text, no letters, no words, no numbers, no digits, no signage lettering, no logos, no badges, no watermarks anywhere in the image. Every sign and panel is completely blank.

THE CHARACTER — the small green car shown in the reference images, exactly as it appears there and nowhere altered. An ordinary everyday Egyptian five-door hatchback with a tall roofline, short overhangs and soft rounded friendly forms. Its paint is flat matte vivid spring-green, one uniform saturated colour, chalky and completely non-glossy — never pale pastel mint. Plain grey five-spoke steel wheels.

ITS FACE — large, round, expressive eyes set INSIDE THE WINDSHIELD GLASS, warm dark irises, calm and slightly weary. Its mouth is its GRILLE: a real recessed slatted grille low in the front bumper whose outline curves gently upward into a closed-mouth smile. The headlamps are ordinary plain lamps and are never eyes. The bodywork below the grille is smooth and blank, with no painted or moulded grin on it.

ITS MARKINGS — the small neat wordmark "140 Auto" in warm off-white on the bonnet and low on each front door, at the size of an ordinary model badge. Its front and rear number-plate recesses are completely EMPTY — blank rectangles with no digits or letters in them. It carries no other text, no numerals, no stripes, no badges and no decals.

WHERE ITS FACE IS — the face exists ONLY on the front of the car. The eyes are in the FRONT windshield and nowhere else. The rear windscreen, the tailgate, the rear bumper and every side window are plain ordinary glass and plain ordinary bodywork with NO eyes, NO mouth and NO face on them. When the camera is behind the car we therefore see only its plain back, with no face visible at all. Never duplicate the face onto the back or the sides.

ONLY THIS CAR IS ALIVE. No other vehicle anywhere in the scene has eyes, a face or any expression. Every other car is an ordinary lifeless vehicle with plain blank headlights and no character whatsoever. Do not give eyes to any other car.

SCENE — Camera is low at road level, BEHIND the little green car, following it as it drives steadily along a dusty Cairo roadside. Along the right kerb stands a scattered group of men in plain grey clothing, car brokers, waving and gesturing at the passing traffic to flag it down. Behind them, propped against a wall and on the kerb, a messy row of blank handwritten cardboard for-sale signs and battered grey second-hand cars parked at careless angles. The green car passes them without slowing, already further up the road. Everything about the roadside is disordered and improvised.
