SCENE — Camera is low at road level, directly BEHIND the little green car, as if following it. We see the back of the green car filling the lower centre of the frame, close to us. Ahead of it a dense queue of stopped grey cars stretches away up the road toward the horizon, brake lights showing as small pale glows. A huge concrete Cairo flyover on round columns crosses overhead above the queue, with another jammed roadway running along the top of it. Dusty concrete, palm trees at the roadside, hazy pale sky. Total gridlock, nothing is moving.
