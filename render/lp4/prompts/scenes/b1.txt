SCENE — Camera is low at road level, BEHIND the little green car, following it as it drives steadily along a dusty Cairo roadside. Along the right kerb stands a scattered group of men in plain grey clothing, car brokers, waving and gesturing at the passing traffic to flag it down. Behind them, propped against a wall and on the kerb, a messy row of blank handwritten cardboard for-sale signs and battered grey second-hand cars parked at careless angles. The green car passes them without slowing, already further up the road. Everything about the roadside is disordered and improvised.
