SCENE — Camera is low at road level, BEHIND the little green car, following it down a wide road on the edge of Cairo. The whole roadside, on both sides and all the way to the horizon, is disordered and improvised: crooked blank cardboard for-sale signs leaning at angles, battered grey second-hand cars parked carelessly at odd angles half on the kerb, broken kerbstones and rubble, tangled overhead wires. The road surface is patched and its lane markings are faded and irregular. Dusty, cluttered, chaotic. The whole frame is uniformly messy with no tidy area anywhere.
