SCENE — Night. A single brightly lit inspection bay at the roadside, open sided, with a clean concrete floor and a plain canopy overhead. One ordinary grey second-hand car stands alone in the middle of the bay, empty and unoccupied. The little green car is at the near edge of the bay, turned side-on in profile, and its headlamps throw two bright cones of light across the grey car, raking over its flank and lighting it up. Everything outside the beams falls away into deep navy darkness. No people anywhere, no tools, no equipment, just the two cars and the light.
