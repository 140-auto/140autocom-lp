SCENE — Camera is low at road level, DIRECTLY BEHIND the little green car and looking the same way it is facing, so we see ONLY THE BACK of the green car — its rear windscreen, tailgate, rear bumper and empty rear number-plate recess. Its face and eyes are on the front and are therefore completely hidden from us in this shot. The green car has slowed and is waiting at a formal institutional checkpoint at the entrance to a walled compound on the edge of the city. The gatehouse is a solid, serious piece of civic architecture: dressed pale stone piers, a deep flat canopy on square columns, a glazed manned booth set into the wall with a uniformed silhouette inside, and a long slim barrier arm across the lane. Everything about it is formal, permanent and administrative — the architecture of an institution that grants or refuses things. It is entirely grey, stone and off-white, and it clearly belongs to somebody other than the little green car. The barrier is lifting, angled upward, the way ahead opening. Hazy pale sky, quiet approach road.
