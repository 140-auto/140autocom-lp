SCENE — Golden hour, downtown Cairo. A wide handsome city square of grand old Khedivial-era apartment buildings with ornate facades, balconies and shuttered windows, opening up around a quiet street. The light is warm but the buildings remain pale off-white and grey. The little green car has pulled up and stopped SHORT OF an empty marked parking bay at the right-hand kerb, angled slightly aside and turned a little toward us so its windshield eyes and grille-smile are visible again, with a warm calm expression. The parking bay ahead of it is clearly EMPTY — its white markings painted on the road surface are fully visible and unobstructed, no car in it. The green car is deliberately holding back and leaving that empty space for whoever is following. Peaceful, generous, end of the journey.
